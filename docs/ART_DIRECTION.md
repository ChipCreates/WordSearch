# Word Sprout art direction

## Visual north star

Word Sprout is a cozy botanical fantasy garden illuminated by gentle bioluminescence.

## Palette and hierarchy

- **Primary:** living leaf green or the theme's brightest botanical accent.
- **Secondary:** moss, water, or warm terracotta for supporting actions.
- **Neutral:** surface, surface-container, and outline tokens for structure.
- **Success:** primary green with a soft glow, never a saturated neon fill.
- **Warning:** warm amber for fertilizer, costs, and attention states.
- **Locked:** readable cool gray-green at reduced saturation; art may dim, text may not.
- **Rarity:** primary for common, secondary/tertiary hues for uncommon and rare, and accent gold only for exceptional tiers.

Gold is reserved for trophies, rare rewards, legendary rarity, and selected premium states. Routine navigation uses the theme primary color.

## Glow tiers

1. **Ambient:** barely visible 0–8px halos behind backgrounds and glass panels.
2. **Interactive:** 0–12px halos on hover, focus, and selected controls.
3. **Reward:** 0–20px halos for discoveries, blooms, achievements, and completed levels.
4. **Legendary:** a restrained layered gold/primary halo for legendary rewards only.

## Material vocabulary

- **Foliage:** soft organic curves, layered greens, and asymmetric leaf shapes.
- **Glass:** translucent surfaces, one quiet border, and a shallow shadow.
- **Aged brass:** warm reward accents with low saturation and small surface area.
- **Soft stone:** neutral containers and readable separators.
- **Water:** cool, calm secondary accents for progress and watering.
- **Light:** localized bioluminescent highlights that explain state or reward.

## Shape, icons, and illustration

- Cards use the shared medium-to-large radius and a single theme-aware border.
- Icon frames are 56–72px, centered, padded, and softly rounded; illustrations fill the frame without cropping.
- Navigation art is transparent, front-facing, and grounded by a soft shadow. Selected navigation is lifted by primary-color light, not gold.
- Plants and badges are bright enough to read at card size. Locked art may be desaturated, but silhouettes remain visible.
- Backgrounds are atmospheric and low contrast behind text. Plant art is the focal point; power-up art keeps the same frame language.

## Type and contrast

- **Quicksand:** headings, labels, and actions; use weight 700–800.
- **Work Sans:** descriptions, helper text, and system feedback.
- **Space Grotesk:** puzzle grid and compact numeric readouts.
- Body text targets WCAG AA contrast (4.5:1); large text targets 3:1. Muted text remains readable against every theme surface.
- Progress labels, locked descriptions, footer text, and cost text must not rely on color alone.

## Responsive composition

At desktop widths the level goal is a compact status row and the board remains dominant. On mobile the board and fixed bottom navigation retain priority, while dialogs and overlays respect safe-area insets. All four themes inherit the same hierarchy; only their palette and atmosphere change.
